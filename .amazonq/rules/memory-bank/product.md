# Product Overview

## Purpose
Institutional website for SLEP Valparaíso (Servicio Local de Educación Pública de Valparaíso) — a Chilean public education service entity. The site is the official digital presence for communicating with citizens, parents, students, and staff.

## Value Proposition
- Official information channel for SLEP Valparaíso under Chile's public education reform (Ley de Nueva Educación Pública)
- Centralizes news, documentation, school listings, and institutional content
- Deployed on Bitnami/NGINX server at https://slepvalparaiso.cl

## Key Features
- **Noticias (News):** Dynamically rendered news cards loaded from a JS data source; individual news detail pages
- **Establecimientos (Schools):** Searchable/filterable directory of schools managed by SLEP Valparaíso
- **Documentación:** Public document library (PDFs) — planning, COSOC minutes, regulations, internal manuals
- **Conócenos / Participación:** Institutional pages about the organization and citizen participation
- **Recursos Internos / Gestor Documental:** Staff-facing resources and document management guides
- **Información Destacada:** Bootstrap carousel with featured banners/links on the homepage
- **OIRS Integration:** Link to external online complaints/suggestions form (Typeform)
- **Transparency links:** Ley de Transparencia, Ley de Lobby, portal-transparencia references

## Target Users
- Citizens and families in the Valparaíso region seeking public education info
- Parents (apoderados) looking for school info or news
- Institutional staff accessing internal resources
- Public officials and oversight bodies (COSOC, Gobierno Regional)
